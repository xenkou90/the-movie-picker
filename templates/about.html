About Follow me on Letterboxd!

I love watching movies and they are my favorite way to relax and escape. However, having seen so many over the years, choosing just one to watch has become a real challenge. I often find myself stuck in endless indecision, spending more time deciding what to watch than actually enjoying the movie. This constant struggle inspired me to create this site to help both myself and others make that choice easier and bring joy back to movie watching.

You can just hit the 'Pick for me!' button or you can upload a CSV file with your watched movies and it will recommend movies you haven't seen yet!
